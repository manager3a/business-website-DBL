# Cookies Policy

**Last updated: September 2026**

This Cookies and Similar Technologies Policy explains how Digital Business Lab LLC ("DBL," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies, local browser storage, and similar technologies on this website.

## 1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may place on your browser or device to support website functionality, remember preferences, or collect information about how a website is used.

Websites may also use similar technologies, including local storage, session storage, pixels, tags, and other browser-based tools. These technologies may perform functions similar to cookies even though they are not technically cookies.

## 2. Technologies We Currently Use

This website currently uses only strictly necessary browser-storage technology to remember whether you have acknowledged the website's cookie and privacy notice.

The preference is stored locally in your browser so that the notice does not need to appear every time you visit the website.

This technology is used only for basic website functionality and does not track your activity across other websites.

The website may also rely on essential technical functionality provided by its hosting, security, and infrastructure providers. These providers may process limited technical information necessary to deliver, protect, and maintain the website.

## 4. Technologies We Do Not Currently Use

## 6. Managing Browser Storage and Cookies

Most browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies and locally stored website information through their privacy or security settings.

You may delete the preference stored by this website at any time by clearing the website's cookies or site data in your browser. If you do so, the cookie and privacy notice may appear again the next time you visit.

Blocking or deleting strictly necessary technologies may affect the website's ability to remember your preferences, although the main website content should generally remain accessible.

Browser controls vary. You should consult your browser's help or settings menu for specific instructions.
